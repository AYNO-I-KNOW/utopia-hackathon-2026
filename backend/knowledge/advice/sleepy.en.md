# Sleepy

Your baby may be tired. Tired babies often rub their eyes, yawn, and become fussy, and can be hard to settle if they stay awake too long.

## Try
- Look for sleepy cues: yawning, rubbing eyes, staring away.
- Dim the lights, lower the noise, and keep movements slow and gentle.
- Swaddle or rock gently if this is part of your routine, and always place your baby on their back to sleep.
- Play soft white noise or a lullaby.

## See a doctor if
- Your baby is hard to wake or is unusually limp or drowsy.
- Breathing looks fast, labored, or irregular.
- Your baby cannot settle for a very long time and seems unwell.
