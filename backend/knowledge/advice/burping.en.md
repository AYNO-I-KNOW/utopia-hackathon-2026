# Needs to burp

Your baby may have swallowed air while feeding and need to burp. Trapped air can make a baby fussy right after or during a feed.

## Try
- Hold your baby upright against your shoulder and gently pat or rub the back.
- Or sit your baby on your lap, supporting the chin and chest, and pat the back.
- Pause during the feed to burp, especially when your baby gets fussy.
- Keep your baby upright for a few minutes after feeding.

## See a doctor if
- Forceful or frequent vomiting after feeds.
- Your baby is not gaining weight or refuses to feed.
- Spit-up that is green, bloody, or looks like coffee grounds.
- Choking, coughing, or breathing trouble during feeds.
