# Uncomfortable

Your baby may be uncomfortable for a reason such as clothing, position, or the room. Small adjustments often help.

## Try
- Check clothing and tags for anything tight, scratchy, or bunched up.
- Change your baby's position and make sure nothing is pressing on the skin.
- Check the room is not too bright, noisy, hot, or cold.
- Check the skin for redness, rashes, or insect bites.

## See a doctor if
- Fever, or your baby feels very hot or very cold.
- Skin rash that spreads or looks unusual.
- Crying that does not stop after you have checked everything.
- Your baby seems to be in pain when touched or moved.
