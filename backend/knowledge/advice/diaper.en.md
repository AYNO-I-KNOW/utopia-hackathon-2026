# Diaper

Your baby may have a wet or dirty diaper. Many babies cry when a diaper is uncomfortable or the skin is irritated.

## Try
- Check the diaper and change it if it is wet or dirty.
- Clean gently and let the skin dry before putting on a new diaper.
- Look for redness or rash and apply a barrier cream if you normally use one.
- Hold your baby for a moment after the change to settle.

## See a doctor if
- Rash that is raw, blistering, or does not improve in a few days.
- Fewer wet diapers than usual.
- Blood in the stool, or very watery or black stools.
- Fever together with diaper rash.
