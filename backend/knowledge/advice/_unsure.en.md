# Not sure yet

The cause is not clear yet. Try checking your baby's basic needs one at a time.

## Try
- Check the diaper and change it if it is wet or dirty.
- Check the time of the last feed and offer a feed if it has been a while.
- Hold and soothe your baby gently, and lower light and noise.

## See a doctor if
- Crying does not stop even after all basic needs are met, or there is a fever.
- Your baby seems unusually sleepy or limp, or feeds very little.
