# Hungry

Your baby may be hungry. Hunger cries are often rhythmic and get louder if feeding is delayed. Babies often show early cues first, such as sucking on hands or turning toward the breast or bottle.

## Try
- Look for hunger cues: sucking on hands, lip smacking, turning the head to search.
- Check the time of the last feed and offer milk if it has been a while.
- Feed calmly and burp your baby during or after the feed.
- If your baby still cries after a full feed, check the diaper and temperature.

## See a doctor if
- Your baby refuses to feed repeatedly or feeds very little.
- Fewer wet diapers than usual.
- Your baby is unusually sleepy, floppy, or hard to wake for feeds.
- Your baby vomits forcefully or often after feeds.
