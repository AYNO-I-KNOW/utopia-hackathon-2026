# Too hot or too cold

Your baby may be too hot or too cold. Babies cannot adjust to temperature as well as adults, so check how they feel and adjust clothing and room.

## Try
- Feel your baby's chest or back of the neck: it should be warm, not sweaty or cold.
- Remove a layer if sweaty or flushed, or add a light layer if cool.
- Keep the room at a comfortable temperature and avoid heavy blankets in the cot.
- Check again after a few minutes.

## See a doctor if
- A temperature of 38 C (100.4 F) or higher in a baby under 3 months, or any fever with a baby who seems unwell.
- Your baby feels very cold or has blue or pale skin.
- Your baby is limp, very sleepy, or hard to wake.
- Fast or labored breathing.
