# Tummy discomfort

Your baby may have gas or tummy discomfort. This is common in young babies and the cry is often strong, with legs pulled up or a tense belly. This app cannot tell whether it is a medical problem.

## Try
- Hold your baby upright and burp after feeds.
- Gently move your baby's legs in a bicycle motion.
- Try a gentle clockwise tummy massage when your baby is calm.
- Check that feeding position and bottle or latch let your baby swallow little air.

## See a doctor if
- Swollen, hard belly or vomiting that is green or bloody.
- Blood in the stool, or no stool with a swollen belly.
- Fever, or your baby is limp or very pale.
- Crying with pain that is severe, constant, or comes in sudden sharp waves.
- Your baby refuses to feed.
