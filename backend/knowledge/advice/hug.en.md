# Wants to be held

Your baby may want closeness and comfort. Young babies often settle when they feel warmth, a heartbeat, and gentle movement.

## Try
- Pick your baby up and hold them close, skin to skin if possible.
- Rock or sway gently and speak or hum softly.
- Try a baby carrier so your baby stays close while your hands are free.
- Keep the room calm and avoid sudden loud noises.

## See a doctor if
- Crying does not ease at all even when held and soothed.
- Your baby looks unwell, feverish, or very pale.
- The cry sounds unusually high-pitched or weak.
