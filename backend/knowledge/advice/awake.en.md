# Awake and wants attention

Your baby may be awake and looking for attention or stimulation, or may be overstimulated. Fussing that is not about hunger or a diaper often settles with a change of activity.

## Try
- Talk, sing, or make eye contact to give gentle attention.
- Change position or location, for example a short walk around the room.
- If the room is bright or noisy, dim the lights and reduce noise.
- Offer tummy time or a simple toy when your baby is calm and awake.

## See a doctor if
- Crying continues for a long time and nothing seems to help.
- Your baby seems unwell, feverish, or unusually irritable.
- Your baby is not responding to you as usual.
